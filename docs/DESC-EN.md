## Full description

```text
SambasKu is a dictionary for Sambas Malay and Indonesian (also known as Kamus Sambas). Search from either language, read the meaning, word class, and an example sentence, and play a pronunciation when the entry has one.

You can bookmark a word and suggest a new entry or a correction. Suggestions are reviewed before they go live.

The app interface is in Indonesian.
```
